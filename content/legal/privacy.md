## 1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Ramsey Diagnostics Corporation d/b/a Labb ("Labb," "we," "us") collects, uses and shares personal information. It covers:

- **labb.com**, including forms, guidebook downloads, newsletters, Quick Test orders, Labb Learning, the Channel Partner Program (CPP) member area and electronic signing (the "Site"); and
- **LabbHR and Labb Station**, the workplace screening platform, app and devices we provide to employers, clinics and other organizations (the "Platform"), together with the [LabbHR Terms of Service](/terms/labbhr).

Use of the Site is also subject to our [Website Terms of Use](/terms). LabbMD and LabbPO are covered by their own terms and privacy notices.

## 2. Information we collect

**Information you give us**

- **Forms and requests:** name, work email, phone, company, title, state, testing volume, your message and the details you choose to share about your program.
- **Newsletter sign-up:** email address and which list you join (employers, clinics, treatment centers or corrections).
- **Quick Test orders:** name, email, phone, shipping address or preferred clinic, the test type, drugs and options you choose, the reason for the test, and who should receive your report. Payment is handled by Stripe, which collects your card details; we receive the payment status, not your card number.
- **Accounts:** login name, password (stored in hashed form), contact details, and for CPP members, clinic and billing information.
- **Labb Learning:** course enrollments, progress, exam results and certificates.
- **Electronic signatures:** the agreement, your typed name, title, company, state of incorporation, and the date, time, IP address and browser used to sign.
- **Communications:** emails, calls and messages you send us.

**Information collected automatically**

- **Site analytics:** pages viewed, time on page, referring site, campaign tags, device type, browser, operating system and country. We use a random visitor identifier stored in your browser and in a first-party cookie to count visits; it does not contain your name or email.
- **Recognizing returning visitors:** when you submit a form on the Site or click a link in an email from Labb, we connect your browser's visitor identifier to your contact record. This lets us see which pages you have viewed on the Site, before and after, so we can follow up about what interests you. Links in our emails carry a coded identifier for this purpose.
- **Company lookup:** we use an IP data provider to identify the organization associated with the network you visit from, such as your employer. We store the organization's name and a coded version of your IP address, not the IP address itself. Visits from home and mobile networks are not matched to a person.
- **Cookies:** cookies that keep you signed in and remember when you have unlocked a guidebook. If we use Google Tag Manager or similar tools, they may set their own cookies to measure traffic and marketing.
- **Email activity:** delivery and bounce information, and where our email provider reports it, opens and clicks, used to send email reliably and measure what readers find useful.
- **Security logs:** IP address and request information, used to protect the Site and prevent abuse.

**Information from others**

- **Labb Station and LabbHR:** contact details of customer users who opt in to Labb email.
- **CRM and marketing records:** contacts who opted in to Labb email through our customer relationship and marketing tools.
- **CPP members:** a member clinic may add contact details for a business it works with, so that the clinic can follow up or send a Labb guidebook.
- **Business sources:** trade shows, referrals and publicly available business contact information.

## 3. Platform and donor information

When an organization uses LabbHR or Labb Station, it may enter or collect information about the people it tests ("donors"), such as name, identifiers, contact details, consent and authorization forms, collection details, images of test devices, and test results. This can include health information.

For this information, Labb acts on behalf of the organization that uses the Platform. The organization decides why donors are tested and who receives results, is responsible for giving donors any required notices and getting their consent, and owns its customer data as described in the LabbHR Terms of Service. Donors with questions about their records should contact the organization that tested them; we will help that organization respond.

We use Platform information to provide, maintain, secure and improve the Platform; to share results with the organization, its authorized users, the collection site and, where ordered, the laboratory and medical review officer performing confirmation testing; and as required by law. We may create de-identified and aggregate data from Platform information and use it for product development, quality, research and benchmarking, as described in the LabbHR Terms of Service. We do not attempt to re-identify de-identified data.

## 4. How we use information

- To respond to requests, send the guidebooks and resources you ask for, and follow up about your program.
- To process and ship Quick Test orders, arrange proctoring or collection, deliver reports to the people you authorize, and provide customer support.
- To provide accounts, courses, certificates, the CPP member area and electronic signing.
- To send newsletters, guidebook email series and other marketing you have agreed to receive.
- To connect you with a Labb Channel Partner clinic that can serve you.
- To measure and improve the Site, our content and our marketing, including understanding which organizations and known contacts visit and what they view.
- To protect the Site, prevent fraud, enforce our terms and meet legal obligations.

## 5. How we share information

We do not sell personal information. We share it only as follows:

- **Service providers** that work for us, such as hosting, payment processing, email delivery, shipping, video proctoring, analytics, IP data lookup, and customer relationship management. They may use the information only to provide their services to us.
- **Channel Partner Program members.** When a CPP member clinic is best placed to serve your request, we share your contact and request details with that clinic. A clinic that sends you a Labb guidebook series receives the replies you send.
- **Laboratories and medical review officers,** when confirmation testing or review is ordered for a test.
- **People you authorize,** such as the employer, attorney or court you choose to receive a Quick Test report.
- **Customer organizations,** for information about their own donors and users on the Platform.
- **Legal and safety reasons,** when required by law or legal process, or to protect the rights, property or safety of Labb, our customers or others.
- **Business transfers,** as part of a merger, sale of assets or similar transaction, subject to this policy.

## 6. Email choices

Every marketing email includes an unsubscribe link. Unsubscribing removes you from that list, and unsubscribing from guidebook emails stops every guidebook series sent to you, including those sent by a CPP member clinic. We will still send messages about orders, accounts and agreements. You can also email info@labb.com to be removed.

## 7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to know what personal information we hold about you, to get a copy, to correct it, to delete it, and to opt out of targeted advertising or the sale or sharing of personal information. To make a request, email info@labb.com or call (877) 868-9967. We will verify your request before acting on it, and you may use an authorized agent. If we deny your request, you may appeal by replying to our decision; we will respond within the time required by law. We will not discriminate against you for using these rights.

To opt out of any sale or sharing of personal information, email us with the subject "Do not sell or share." You can also block or delete cookies and site data in your browser settings, which resets the visitor identifier and ends any link between your browser and your contact record; some parts of the Site may not work without cookies. You can ask us to delete your website activity by emailing info@labb.com.

Requests about donor records on the Platform should go to the organization that tested you.

## 8. Retention

We keep personal information as long as needed for the purposes described in this policy, including to provide services, keep business and tax records, resolve disputes and meet legal obligations. Signed agreements and order records are kept for the life of the relationship and as required afterward. Platform records are kept as described in the LabbHR Terms of Service and the customer's instructions.

## 9. Security

We use administrative, technical and physical safeguards designed to protect personal information, including encrypted connections, access controls and hashed passwords. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee security. If we learn of a security incident that affects your personal information, we will notify you as required by law.

## 10. Children

The Site is intended for adults and organizations. Quick Test orders are available only to people 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through the Site. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

## 11. Where information is stored

Labb operates in the United States, and information is stored and processed in the United States.
